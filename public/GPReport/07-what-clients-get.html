What This Means For Clients

A system that reads like a thoughtful analyst, narrows like a scientist, and preserves the user's final say.

The client gets a guided investigation, not a black box.

The system combines semantic search, model judgment, facet discovery, and smart questions to make large archives feel reviewable.

Finds what keywords miss

Meaning-based retrieval can surface subtle tweets that never use the user's exact concern words.

Explains why

Candidates carry source families, judge reasons, facet labels, examples, and model provenance.

Asks fewer, better questions

The system looks for boundary questions that move clusters, not individual tweets.

Keeps the user in control

Models narrow and explain. The user approves boundaries and makes final decisions.

The implication is powerful: a personal archive can become a navigable reasoning space where one good question replaces a long, exhausting manual pass.

Future stages apply approved answers as concern-local rules and build an auditable review package. The system still avoids accuracy claims until human labels are imported and measured.
