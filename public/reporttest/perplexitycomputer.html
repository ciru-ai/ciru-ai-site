Ethics And Risk

Trust design is part of the product

Data handling

Do not store sensitive NHS, benefit, bill, or financial data unless the service truly needs it. Consent has to be plain and informed.

AI failure

Official forms, health, benefits, legal, and money decisions need human verification. AI output must be framed as fallible.

Safeguarding

DBS checks, escalation routes, verified identity, and no unsolicited payment requests are baseline controls, not nice-to-haves.

Minimum Viable Pilot

Run a 12-week proof point before scaling

The report recommends 6-8 scam-safety plus AI-confidence cohorts for a single housing association or council area, with printed materials and phone/WhatsApp follow-up.

First paid offer

  • Buyer: housing association or council inclusion lead.
  • Offer: AI Scam Safety + Digital Confidence cohort.
  • Price: £800-£1,800 per cohort.
  • Delivery: trusted local venue, printed guide, phone/WhatsApp follow-up.
  • Proof: satisfaction, confidence gain, renewal intent, funder anecdotes.

Decision thresholds

  • Upgrade: buyers pay over £1,500/cohort with annual renewal.
  • Upgrade: bank or telecom signs an AI-specific CSR partnership.
  • Upgrade: family help-desk trial-to-paid conversion clears 20% with low churn.
  • Downgrade: no-show or attrition exceeds 40%.
  • Downgrade: institutional sales cycles exceed 9-12 months with low close rates.
Closing verdict: this can be a worthwhile mission-led business, but it is not a venture-scale consumer app. Build for the payer, frame for the user's fears and needs, lead with safety, and keep AI quiet until it solves a concrete problem.
