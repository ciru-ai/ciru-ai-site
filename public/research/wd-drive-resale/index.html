Inventory reality

The 16TB pool drives the financial result

The 39 × 16TB units are 49% of the drive count, 55% of raw capacity, and roughly 56% of gross receipts in the three main scenarios. Their current market is also the least stable: April sold prices clustered near $250 delivered, while one July sale reached $363.50 and live asks sit sharply above that older sold-price cluster.

Implication: price the first few 16TB drives as a discovery batch, not all 39 from one optimistic comp.

The cash bands are independent-extreme envelopes: they pair the lowest price with the longest duration and the highest price with the shortest duration. Real price and speed are likely inversely related, so the midpoint lines are the better planning view. These are judgment ranges, not confidence intervals, and every scenario holds back the same conservative 5% claims reserve. “Duration-adjusted” means Store carrying only; it is not a discounted-cash-flow calculation.

Long timelines do not mean every drive sits that long. The 12TB and 14TB pools may clear much earlier; the finish date is controlled by the 39-unit 16TB position. The aggressive floor is modeled at 120 months; every additional year of continuous Basic Store membership subtracts another $263.40. Future market prices can change enough that a multi-year projection becomes less reliable.

Turn the forecast into measured data

Launch two units per capacity at the target price for 30 days. Track impressions, views, watchers, offers, accepted price, and days to sale; replenish only after a sale. Reforecast after six weeks or ten completed sales. Keep only 2–4 units per capacity/grade visibly available so the listing does not advertise oversupply.

Basic Store is worth it

eBay’s current U.S. hard-drive rate is 7.35% + $0.40/order for a Basic-or-higher Store versus 13.6% + $0.40/order without a qualifying Store. The Store clearly pays during the active high-volume phase, but review it annually: at roughly $3,939 delivered sales per year, the annual Basic subscription and percentage-fee savings break even.

Fee assumptions

The fee model assumes a U.S., Above Standard seller, the correct Hard Drives (HDD, SSD & NAS) category, domestic buyers, no promoted ads, no international fee, and a 7% blended buyer sales-tax assumption solely because eBay assesses its percentage fee on tax too. Below Standard or elevated “item not as described” status can add seller-level surcharges.

FVF estimate = delivered pre-tax receipts × 7.35% × (1 + 7% assumed buyer tax) + $0.40 × 79 orders. Shipping charged to buyers is included in delivered receipts, then the matching label budget is deducted. Selling plan

Maximize trust without flooding the market

RECOMMENDED EXECUTION

Build a serial ledger

Record capacity, product family, enclosure model, serial, power-adapter match, cosmetic grade, warranty status, power-on hours, and SMART attributes for every unit.

Wipe and prove health

Complete a full read/long test, securely erase prior data, then capture a fresh SMART screenshot showing serial, hours, reallocated sectors, pending sectors, and uncorrectable errors.

Grade into real condition bands

A: low-hour/clean; B: healthy normal-hour; C: high-hour or cosmetic wear. Remove any drive with pending, reallocated, or uncorrectable sectors from the healthy pricing model.

Release 2–4 per capacity

First check the account’s Seller Hub selling limit. Use one multi-quantity fixed-price listing per genuinely identical grade/capacity, then replenish quietly. eBay discourages duplicate fixed-price listings, and a visible 39-unit quantity advertises oversupply.

Listing title pattern

WD [Elements/easystore] [12/14/16]TB External HDD · SMART Tested · OEM Power + USB · Box

Use photos of the exact contents and the matching serial/SMART proof. State power-on hours and every cosmetic defect. Do not promise “low hours,” warranty, 7200 RPM, CMR, or a specific internal white-label model unless verified unit by unit.

Packing

The retail box is presentation, not the shipping carton. Put it inside a strong outer box with immobilizing padding. Budget the model’s destination-sensitive $18.50 average label and $4.50 materials per unit; charge calculated shipping and purchase appropriate declared value or third-party coverage above the carrier’s included protection.
