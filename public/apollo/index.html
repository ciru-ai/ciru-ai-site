What we tried

The route from scalar Q2 to targeted rescue

Scalar Q2 controlEstablished the traditional four-level baseline and the damage a practical Q2 expert format must beat.
Uniform VQ4D · 2.2505 bpwEncoded four weights jointly with one 8-bit codebook index and a G32 Scale8 scale. It clearly beat scalar Q2 and was close to Antirez.
Four-bank VQ4D · 2.2665 bpwKept two-bit indices and added a 2-bit bank selector per G32. It improved reconstruction and slightly passed Antirez, but did not close enough of the quality gap.
Q4-rescue63 · 2.74292 bpwKept VQ4D for 193 experts and used native signed-IU4 Q4 only for the 63 experts with the most route-weighted output error. This produced the decisive improvement.

Early unclamped results were discarded. All evidence reported below uses the official gate clamp ≤10 and up clamp [−10, 10].
