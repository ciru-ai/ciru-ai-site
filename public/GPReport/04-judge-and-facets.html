The 35B Judge And The Facet Lens

The large model reviews candidate packets, then the system groups the answers into explainable concern patterns.

The 35B judge turns candidates into evidence.

Qwen3.6 35B reads candidate packets and assigns a concern-match bucket. It also explains why a tweet may match, why it may be a false positive, and what ambiguity remains.

BucketMeaningCurrent example count
Likely matchThe candidate appears to match the concern from visible evidence.3
Possible matchThe candidate may match, but the boundary or context is unclear.20
Needs contextThe visible text is not enough, often because the tweet is a reply or link.3
Likely false positiveThe candidate was retrieved but probably does not match the concern.100

The system then groups those judgments into concern facets. A facet is a pattern like social interaction, contextless link, technical false positive, or financial topic mismatch.

Facets are the microscope: instead of staring at individual tweets, the system sees repeating shapes in the uncertainty.
