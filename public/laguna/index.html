STEP 1

🎪 Check Your Magic Computer Box! 🎪

Hey hey hey! Before we make the magic happen, let's make sure your computer is ready for the big show! 🎪

🎂 Birthday Requirement: You need a computer with AMD Ryzen AI Max "Strix Halo" - that's the fancy name for your birthday present! 🎁

🖥️➡️🎮➡️🚀

Check your system:

uname -r # Should show kernel 6.18.4 or newer! # Kernels older than 6.18.4 are BAD! They make the magic crash! 💥
cat /proc/cpuinfo | grep "Strix Halo" # If you see text, your magic box is ready! ✨
⚠️ CLOWN WARNING: Do NOT use linux-firmware-20251125! It breaks everything! 💥 Use linux-firmware-20260110 or newer!

🎂 Birthday Boot Parameters (add these to make it FAST!):

amd_iommu=off amdgpu.gttsize=126976 ttm.pages_limit=32505856

These tell your computer to share ALL its memory with the GPU! Like sharing your birthday cake with everyone! 🎂

STEP 3

🎪 Summon Your Magic Toolbox! 🎪

Now we summon the magic toolbox! It's like calling a clown to your birthday party! 🎪

⬇️ ⬇️ ⬇️

Option A: Vulkan (RADV/AMDVLK) - Best for compatibility!

toolbox create llama-vulkan-radv \--image docker.io/kyuz0/amd-strix-halo-toolboxes:vulkan-radv \-- --device /dev/dri --group-add video --security-opt seccomp=unconfined

Option B: ROCm (Recommended for Performance!)

toolbox create llama-rocm-7.2.4 \--image docker.io/kyuz0/amd-strix-halo-toolboxes:rocm-7.2.4 \-- --device /dev/dri --device /dev/kfd --group-add video --group-add render --group-add sudo --security-opt seccomp=unconfined
🦙🦙🦙

Ubuntu users! Use distrobox instead of toolbox! 🐧

distrobox create llama-rocm-7.2.4 \--image docker.io/kyuz0/amd-strix-halo-toolboxes:rocm-7.2.4 \-- --device /dev/dri --device /dev/kfd --group-add video --group-add render --group-add sudo --security-opt seccomp=unconfined

Then enter your magic toolbox! 🎩✨

toolbox enter llama-vulkan-radv
STEP 4

🎪 Check That Your GPU is Waving Hello! 🎪

Let's make sure your GPU is ready to party! 🎉

🎮✨🎮
llama-cli --list-devices

If you see your GPU listed, it's party time! 🎊

⚠️ CLOWN WARNING: If you see "No devices found", check your --device flags! You need /dev/dri and /dev/kfd for ROCm!

Quick GPU check:

ls -la /dev/dri/ # You should see card0 and renderD128! ls -la /dev/kfd # This is the ROCm magic door! 🚪
STEP 5

🎪 Download Your AI Birthday Present! 🎪

Time to download your AI model! It's like getting presents on your birthday! 🎁🎁🎁

⬇️ ⬇️ ⬇️

HF_XET_HIGH_PERFORMANCE=1 hf download unsloth/Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B-Instruct-GGUF \--local-dir models/qwen3-coder-30B-A3B/

Pro tip: Set your Hugging Face token for faster downloads! 🚀

STEP 6

🎪 Make the Magic Happen! Run Your AI! 🎪

OKAY! THE BIG MOMENT! Let's make the AI go BRRRRR! 🚀🚀🚀

🎮⚡🎮
⚠️ CLOWN WARNING: ALWAYS use -fa 1 (flash attention) and --no-mmap! Without these, the magic crashes! 💥💥💥

Option A: Server Mode (API) - Like a web server for your AI! 🌐

llama-server -m models/qwen3-coder-30B-A3B/BF16/Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B-Instruct-BF16-00001-of-00002.gguf \-c 8192 -ngl 999 -fa 1 --no-mmap

Option B: CLI Mode - Chat directly with your AI! 💬

llama-cli --no-mmap -ngl 999 -fa 1 \<-m models/qwen3-coder-30B-A3B/BF16/Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B-Instruct-BF16-00001-of-00002.gguf \<-p "Write a Strix Halo toolkit haiku."
🦙🎉🦙🎉🦙

Router Mode (multi-model routing):

llama-server --models-preset models.ini --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8080 --models-max 1 --parallel 1
