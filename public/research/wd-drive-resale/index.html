Execution

Prove health, stage supply, and test the new 16TB ceiling

RECOMMENDED LAUNCH

Build a serial ledger

Record capacity, family, enclosure model, serial, matching adapter, cosmetic grade, warranty status, power-on hours, and SMART attributes for all 79 units.

Wipe and prove health

Complete a long/full read test, securely erase prior data, then capture a fresh serial-matched SMART screenshot. Pull any drive with pending, reallocated, or uncorrectable sectors out of the healthy model.

Launch small cohorts

Start with roughly 3×12TB, 3×14TB, and 4×16TB. Use multi-quantity listings only for genuinely identical grade/family units; replenish quietly after sales rather than displaying all 79.

Reprice from actual outcomes

After the first 8–10 completed 16TB orders, compare watchers, offers, days-to-sale, return rate, and net. Raise or lower the acceptance floor—do not let one $462.86 completion-page display dictate all 39.

Packing standard

Method and limits

What the evidence can—and cannot—support

RESEARCH SNAPSHOT · JUL 20
Included
  • US eBay used/open-box WD Elements and easystore desktop external drives matching 12TB, 14TB, or 16TB.
  • Dated completion pages and explicitly labeled final out-of-stock listing states with visible item price and shipping for listing-level price statistics.
  • Multi-quantity sold counters as physical-unit floors, kept separate from price observations.
  • Current used/open-box ask snapshots for negotiation context, with extreme asks labeled.
Excluded or caveated
  • New, manufacturer-refurbished, internal/bare, My Book, RAID, defective, and non-WD comparables.
  • Hidden Best Offer settlements are not treated as exact accepted prices.
  • Sold counters do not reveal every unit date or historical price; one counter snapshot cannot establish a rate.
  • Repeated states can show a counter delta, but revision dates are only directional brackets—not a transaction ledger.

Marketplace pages are dynamic. eBay can relist, localize, revise, or eventually remove ended pages; shipping can vary by destination. The report records what the page or indexed state displayed during the July 20 audit. It is an estimate, not a guarantee of price, sale time, or tax treatment.
