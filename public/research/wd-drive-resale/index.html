The 16TB pool drives the financial result

The 39 × 16TB units are 49% of the drive count, 55% of raw capacity, and roughly 56–60% of modeled gross receipts. Their current market is also the least stable: April sold prices clustered near $250 delivered, while one July sale reached $363.50 and live asks moved sharply higher.

Basic Store is worth it

eBay’s current U.S. hard-drive rate is 7.35% + $0.40/order for a Basic-or-higher Store versus 13.6% + $0.40/order without a qualifying Store. In the normal case, the modeled Basic Store saves about $1,309 net even after six monthly subscriptions.

Fee assumptions

The fee model assumes a U.S., Above Standard seller, the correct Hard Drives (HDD, SSD & NAS) category, domestic buyers, no promoted ads, no international fee, and a 7% blended buyer sales-tax assumption solely because eBay assesses its percentage fee on tax too. Below Standard or elevated “item not as described” status can add seller-level surcharges.

FVF estimate = delivered pre-tax receipts × 7.35% × (1 + 7% assumed buyer tax) + $0.40 × 79 orders. Shipping charged to buyers is included in delivered receipts, then the matching label budget is deducted.

Selling plan

Maximize trust without flooding the market

RECOMMENDED EXECUTION

Build a serial ledger

Record capacity, product family, enclosure model, serial, power-adapter match, cosmetic grade, warranty status, power-on hours, and SMART attributes for every unit.

Wipe and prove health

Complete a full read/long test, securely erase prior data, then capture a fresh SMART screenshot showing serial, hours, reallocated sectors, pending sectors, and uncorrectable errors.

Grade into real condition bands

A: low-hour/clean; B: healthy normal-hour; C: high-hour or cosmetic wear. Remove any drive with pending, reallocated, or uncorrectable sectors from the healthy pricing model.

Release 2–4 per capacity

First check the account’s Seller Hub selling limit. Use one multi-quantity fixed-price listing per genuinely identical grade/capacity, then replenish quietly. eBay discourages duplicate fixed-price listings, and a visible 39-unit quantity advertises oversupply.

Listing title pattern

WD [Elements/easystore] [12/14/16]TB External HDD · SMART Tested · OEM Power + USB · Box

Use photos of the exact contents and the matching serial/SMART proof. State power-on hours and every cosmetic defect. Do not promise “low hours,” warranty, 7200 RPM, CMR, or a specific internal white-label model unless verified unit by unit.

Packing

The retail box is presentation, not the shipping carton. Put it inside a strong outer box with immobilizing padding. Budget the model’s destination-sensitive $18.50 average label and $4.50 materials per unit; charge calculated shipping and purchase appropriate declared value or third-party coverage above the carrier’s included protection.
